How the checks work

Thickness

  • Width is measured with a rolling disk at exactly 0.5 mm, with no slack. If no 0.5 mm disk fits anywhere over a spot, that spot is too thin.
  • Spots under 1 mm are traced along their centre line. If one runs 10 mm or longer, it fails.
  • Gaps between pieces get the same test, since a 0.3 mm gap won't weed either.
  • Letters are DIN Condensed Bold, run through the same checker at 10 mm tall (the minimum). Strokes there are already 1.4 mm, so only sharp inside notches change.
  • Sharp corners always taper to zero, so a tip shorter than 0.6 mm counts as a corner. Anything longer counts as a sliver.

What changed in the art

  • Broncobots: the traced gear was replaced with a clean 13-tooth gear fitted to the original. The horse shading became cut-outs.
  • BrophySat-1: the colours collapse to one. Version B keeps only the line work and adds round stars.
  • Long hairlines were thickened to 1.1 mm. Gaps under 0.6 mm were closed and slivers under 0.6 mm removed.

About the sliders

  • Logos only shrink as far as their measured minimum, found by stepping each one down 2.5% at a time until the strict check fails. Below that, the editor switches to a version re-thickened at 125 mm.
  • Every scaled path is re-baked into the exported file, with no transforms left for the cutter to misread.
  • The verified files are the ground truth. If you export a new layout, have me run it through the checker before cutting.
